# @engine/gameplay — Survival Systems Design (v2)

This version covers base building, an expanded resource and refining economy, combat, and zombies, on top of the v1 systems: vitals, inventory, crafting, gathering and environment.
Read it with `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (core batches 2 and 3), `docs/DECISIONS.md` and `packages/net/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

**Ownership.**
- **Visuals belong to Art & Assets**: sprites, animations, VFX, SFX, UI, colours and palettes.
- Gameplay exposes only numeric, render-facing fields: `BuildPiece.{type, tier, variant, paint, damageStage, rot}`, `AnimState`, `Zombie.kind`, `BuildGhost`, `Fuel.lit`, `DoorState.open`.
- Every def carries a **manifest key** (`sprite`, defaulting to the def key). `visualId` is resolved from those keys through the `@engine/assets` registry (`resolveSprite`). Gameplay stores no paths, colours or styling.
- `paint` is an index into an Art-owned palette.

## 0. Engine rules this package follows

- **Components** are numeric SoA schemas. Inventories use array fields (`{type:"u16", length:N}`, accessed with `getArray`/`setElement`), and schemas declare `{default}`.
- **No wall clock and no `Math.random`.**
  - Outcomes that must agree across peers use `world.rngFor(key, netId)`: gather yields, zombie wander and zombie variants.
  - Authority-only rolls use `world.rng`: weather, spawn placement.
- **Server-authoritative.**
  - The client predicts its own vitals, stamina, cooldowns, crafting progress, the placement **ghost**, and its attack swing.
  - It never predicts placement, damage, destruction, AI or spawning.
- **Replay guard.** Systems with side effects check `world.isReplaying`. Core also discards events emitted during replay. One-shot actions are cleared at the end of the simulation phase.
- **Input.**
  - `GameplayActions = defineActions([...])` (adds `Sprint`).
  - `GAMEPLAY_COMMANDS` are core `defineCommand` schemas. `COMMAND_ENTITY_FIELDS` lists the NetId fields that net resolves to Entities before `applyGameplayInput`.
- **Ids.** Items, resources, recipes, pieces and zombies use stable u16 ids from `StableIdRegistry` plus `data/ids.lock.json`. The lock is append-only; regenerate it with `pnpm --filter @engine/gameplay lock:ids`. A test fails if the lock is stale. Previously shipped ids are preserved.
- **Range checks** use `reach + slack`, where `slack = playerMaxSpeed × min(rtt/2, 0.2 s)`. `rtt` is read from the `Latency` component, which net writes server-side.
- **Events** are tick-stamped (`tick` in every payload) and go through core `EventBus`. Net chooses reliable or unreliable per event (§7).

## 3. Building (base building)

### 3.1 Model
- **One entity per piece**: grid-snapped `Transform`, `BuildPiece`, optional `Tribe`, plus feature components (`DoorState`, `Fuel`, `Container`, `CraftingStation`, `HeatSource`, `PrivilegeZone`).
- **Grid.** One tile per building cell, with levels `z` 0..7 (`levelHeight = 2` tile units of Transform.z).
- **Cell pieces** (foundation, floor, hatch frame, roofs, stairs, pillar, furniture, decor) live in `@engine/world` `BuildingGrid` on their layer: 0 deck, 1 structure, 2 furniture, 3 decor.
- **Edge pieces** (walls) sit **between** tiles. They live in gameplay's `EdgeIndex` keyed by (tx, ty, z, side N|W). The edge midpoint is the Transform.
- **Opening pieces** (doors, shutters, window glass/bars, hatches) mount into a parent's socket and live in the `OpeningIndex`, keyed by parent piece.
- Gameplay keeps the maps `gridId ↔ entity`, plus per-chunk piece sets and a **chunk version** (bumped on every structural change in a chunk) for static replication and persistence.

### 3.2 Placement
- **Command:** `Place {piece, x, y, level, rot, tier, variant}`, carrying aim in world units.
- **`snapPlacement`:**
  - **cell:** tile = floor(aim), rot 0..3.
  - **edge:** nearest `EDGE` socket of a deck at that level (through `BuildingGrid.snap`). Without a deck, the nearest edge of the aimed tile. rot is derived from the side.
  - **opening:** nearest free socket of the matching kind (door, window, hatch) on a parent.
  - **free:** aim snapped to 1/4 tile, rot in 16 steps (Transform.facing). Occupies its footprint tiles on its layer.
- **Validation** (`validatePlacement` → `PlaceFail`, in this order):
  1. `UnknownPiece`, `BadTier`
  2. `OutOfRange` (reach + slack)
  3. `LevelLimit`
  4. `NoPrivilege`: the point is inside another tribe's `PrivilegeZone`, or a totem's zone would overlap another tribe's zone
  5. `ZoneFull` (5,000 pieces per zone)
  6. `Terrain`: unbuildable tile, or slope above `maxSlope` for foundations and ground furniture
  7. `Occupied` (grid, edge or opening index)
  8. `Blocked`: a creature or resource node in the footprint (spatial index)
  9. `NoSocket`
  10. `NoSupport`: a deck is required, or the computed support is below `minStability`
  11. `CannotAfford`
- **Effects.** On success the cost is consumed, the entity is spawned and indexed, stability is marked dirty, noise is made, and `PiecePlaced` is emitted. Otherwise `BuildFailed{reason}` is emitted.
- **Ghost.** On the client the `gameplay.ghost` system runs `previewPlacement` (the same snap and validate, minus server-only stability values) each frame tick, for entities with `BuildGhost`. Renderers draw the ghost from `BuildGhost`. Placement itself is never predicted.

### 3.3 Support and stability (server only; `PieceStability` never replicates)
- **Directed support graph:**
  - `foundation` and ground furniture are grounded (100).
  - `wall@z` is supported by decks at z on either side, or by `wall@z-1`.
  - `deck@z≥1` is supported by walls at z-1 on its edges, by `supportsAbove` pieces below (pillars; grid `supportsOf`), or by adjacent decks at z (horizontal).
  - `stairs` and `pillar` are supported by the deck at z.
  - Furniture and decor on a deck rest on that deck (no loss).
  - Openings rest on their parent (no loss).
- **Recompute.** Value = max over supporters of (supporter − loss), where loss is the receiving piece's tier `vertLoss` or `horizLoss`. It is computed as a max-Dijkstra with a bucket queue (values 0..100) over the dirty piece set: an O(n) flood.
- **Hook.** It plugs into `BuildingGrid.stabilityRule`, so the rule returns `value ≥ minStability` and `grid.unstable(ids)` yields the collapse set.
- **Collapse.** Pieces below the minimum are destroyed (`PieceDestroyed{cause: Collapse}`), and the flood repeats until no more pieces fall.

### 3.4 HP, tiers, upgrades, repair, demolition
- **Tiers** (tiers.json): twig, wood, stone, metal, reinforced. hp = base × `hpMul`. Cost = tier cost × `costScale`, rounded up.
- **Damage stage** comes from the hp fraction (thresholds 0.75 / 0.5 / 0.25 → stages 0..3) and is shown by Art.
- **Damage is batched.** `damagePiece()` adds to a per-tick pending map, and `gameplay.structureDamage` writes `BuildPiece.hp` at most once per piece per tick. It then emits one `PieceDamaged` per piece and destroys pieces at 0.
- **`Upgrade {target, tier}`** needs privilege and a higher allowed tier. It pays the new tier's cost and keeps the hp fraction.
- **`Repair {target}`** costs ceil(missing fraction × tier cost × `repairMul`). It is blocked for `repairBlockedSeconds` after damage.
- **`Demolish {target}`** needs privilege and refunds `demolishRefund` × hp fraction of the cost. Dependents re-evaluate stability.
- **`Paint {target, paint}`** needs a `paintable` piece and costs 1 dye. `variant` is chosen at placement (cycled by command).

### 3.5 Doors, containers, privilege
- **Doors.** `ToggleDoor {target}` works within reach + slack. If `lockTribe ≠ 0`, only that tribe can open the door. `LockDoor {target}` (with privilege) toggles the lock. A closed door blocks pathing; an open one does not. Doors emit noise.
- **Containers.**
  - `OpenContainer {target}` checks range, privilege (inside a zone only the owning tribe may open), and viewer capacity (8). It adds `Owner.clientId` to `ContainerViewers` and sets the player's `Viewing`.
  - `CloseContainer` reverses that. Viewers are also removed automatically when they leave `containerReach + slack`, die, or the container is destroyed.
  - `Deposit` and `Withdraw {target, item, count}` require an open view.
  - Net uses `containerViewersScope` (`(world, e, clientId) → bool`) as the `viewers` scope for `Container`.
- **Privilege.** `claim_totem` adds `PrivilegeZone {radius 12, tribe}`. Inside a zone, only that tribe may build, upgrade, demolish, lock, or open containers. Players and pieces carry `Tribe`.

### 3.6 Persistence
- `encodeStructures(world, chunkKey)` / `decodeStructures(world, bytes)` (`src/building/persist.ts`). This is gameplay's versioned, deterministic codec.
- **Header:** magic `STRC`, u16 version, i32 cx, cy, u32 piece-id hash, u32 item-id hash, u32 count.
- **Pieces** are sorted by (z, ty, tx, layer, side, key). Each record stores: type, rot, variant, tier, paint, hp, chunk-relative x/y as i16 in 1/4 tiles, z, tribe, and a feature bitmask with DoorState / Fuel / Container contents.
- **Decode.** The decoder rejects newer versions and hash mismatches. It recreates entities, reindexes them, and recomputes stability. Re-encoding a decoded chunk gives byte-identical output, which a test checks.
- **Integration.** Apps store the blob next to the engine chunk bytes. `attachWorldStreaming(world, chunkManager, storage)` does this on `onUnload` / `onLoad`.

### 3.7 Events
`PiecePlaced, BuildFailed{action, reason}, PieceDamaged{piece, hp, amount}, PieceDestroyed{piece, type, cause}, PieceUpgraded, PieceRepaired, PieceDemolished, PiecePainted, DoorToggled, ContainerOpened, ContainerClosed`.

### 3.8 Render-facing (Art & Assets reads)
- `BuildPiece.type` → piece manifest key. `tier` gives the material, `variant` the alternate model, `paint` the palette index (Art-owned), and `damageStage` 0..3.
- `rot` (0..3, or 16 steps through Transform.facing for free pieces) and `DoorState.open`.
- `Fuel.lit` and `HeatSource.lit` drive fire/light VFX.
- `BuildGhost`: `valid` plus `reason`.

## 4. Resources and refining

- **Nodes** (resources.json): `fallen_branches, fiber_grass, berry_bush, loose_rocks, sand_dune, clay_deposit, tree (pine), oak_tree, boulder, copper_vein, tin_vein, iron_vein, coal_seam, animal_carcass`.
  - Each node has a tool kind (none, axe, pickaxe, shovel, knife) and a min tier (stone 1, bronze 2, iron 3).
  - Each also has yields, capacity, respawn time, cooldown, and stamina cost.
- **World integration.** `worldgenBands(defs)` turns `data/worldgen.json` (tile ids plus node keys per band) into `ProceduralGenerator` bands. `attachWorldStreaming` spawns `ResourceNode` entities from `ChunkManager.onLoad(chunk, nodes)` and despawns them on unload. It is idempotent per chunk.
- **Items** (items.json): logs, sticks, planks, stone, flint, clay, sand, fiber, hide, ores (copper, tin, iron), coal, and refined goods: rope, cloth, leather, stone_brick, clay_brick, mortar, charcoal, the ingots, nails, plates, glass, dye. Also food, bandage, tools (stone, bronze, iron) and weapons. Fuel items carry `fuelSeconds`.
- **Recipes** (recipes.json), 34 in total, at stations: hand, workbench, campfire, furnace (fired) and anvil. Stations are build pieces. Fired stations need `Fuel.lit`.
- **Fuel** (`gameplay.fuel`, server): a lit station burns `burnTicks`. At 0 it consumes the first fuel item in its `Container`, or goes out (`FuelOut`). `ToggleFire {target}` lights it or puts it out.
- **Replication.** Nodes replicate to all. Yields roll with `rngFor("gather", nodeNetId)` on the server, and items reach the owner through the Inventory.
- **Events:** `ItemGathered, GatherSwing, GatherFailed, NodeDepleted, NodeRespawned, InventoryOverflow, FuelOut, FireToggled`.

## 5. Combat and zombies

### 5.1 Combat (basic)
- **`Attack {target}`.**
  - Checks: `Attacker.cooldownUntilTick`, then range = weapon range (fists 1.0) + slack, then stamina.
  - Damage = weapon damage (fists 5), applied with `applyDamage(target, amount, cause Combat, source)`.
  - Attacking a piece calls `damagePiece(amount × structureMul)`.
- **Prediction.** The client predicts the swing, cooldown and stamina only. Damage is applied only by the server (not during replay).
- **Death.** Health reaching 0 → `Dead` (existing death system) → `Died`. Zombie corpses despawn after `corpseSeconds`.

### 5.2 Spawning (server, `gameplay.zombieSpawner`)
- Every `checkSeconds`, for each living player, the expected spawn count is computed from `ratePerMinute[phase] × (1 + noiseRateMul × NoiseLevel) × threatLevel()`, with a fractional chance for the remainder.
- Spawning is capped by `cap[phase]` zombies within `capRadius`.
- **Placement.** Position is on a ring of `minDistance..maxDistance`, rolled with `world.rng`, rejecting unwalkable tiles, pieces and privilege zones. Up to 4 tries.
- **Kind** is weighted by phase. Variant comes from `rngFor("zvariant", entity)`.
- **Noise.** Actions call `makeNoise(x, y, radius)`: gather, build, attack, structure hits, doors. This raises `NoiseLevel` for players within the radius (it decays at `decayPerSec`), and the spatial noise points draw nearby zombies.

### 5.3 AI (server, `gameplay.zombieAI`)
- **States:** `Wander → Investigate (noise) → Chase (player in sight) → AttackPlayer | AttackStructure`.
- **Daylight.** In daylight zombies take `dayDps` and give up investigating.
- **Thinking** is staggered (`thinkTicks`, by entity index). Targets are found with `SpatialIndex.queryRadius`.
- **Pathing.**
  - A* on the tile grid within `maxSearchRadius`, with deterministic tie-breaks and a cap on expanded nodes. Unwalkable tiles and solid cell pieces (`BuildingGrid.isBlocked`) are impassable.
  - **Walls and closed doors are passable at a break cost** of `hp / (structureDamage / attackCooldown) × speed × breakCostMul` tile-equivalents. Open doorways are free.
  - The path therefore goes through the cheapest barrier. Doors (low hp) are naturally preferred over stone walls.
  - The first barrier on the path becomes `blockPiece`. The zombie walks to it and enters `AttackStructure`, which hits the piece through `damagePiece` (batched).
  - When the barrier falls, the zombie repaths.
- **Attacking players** happens only when no barrier lies between them.

### 5.4 Render-facing
- `Zombie.kind` gives the manifest key, and `Zombie.variant` the variant.
- `AnimState.anim` is one of Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Hurt, Die, Gather, Build. `AnimState.startTick` is the animation start, for phase. Transform.facing gives direction.
- Health fraction drives the cute damage look; Art decides how.

### 5.5 Events
`ZombieSpawned, ZombieStateChanged (debug), Damaged{target, source, amount, cause}, AttackSwing, Died`.

## 6. Existing systems (v1; still accurate)

- **Vitals:**
  - decay and modifiers; stamina drain, then `staminaDelayTicks` (u16, replicated to the owner), then regen;
  - body temperature;
  - damage from starvation, dehydration and hypothermia/hyperthermia;
  - death.
- **Inventory:** fixed slots. It is now array fields (`items[]`/`counts[]`). Ops are generic over `Inventory` and `Container`.
- **Crafting:** validates at start and exchanges atomically at completion. Stations are found through the spatial index, and fired stations must be lit.
- **Environment:** TimeOfDay and Weather live on `world.environment`. Exposure computes felt temperature (heat sources through the spatial index), wetness and modifiers. `sheltered` is set automatically when a roof or deck exists above the entity's tile.

### 6.1 Heat sources and windsock (slice: fireplace / wood_stove / windsock)
- `fireplace` (2×1, tier 2, heat 22 r5) and `wood_stove` (tier 3, heat 16 r4, cooks as campfire) are `fired` pieces: Fuel + fuel Container + heat Emitter + light, lit via ToggleFire like campfires.
- `pieces.json fuelMul` (default 1): each fuel item burns `fuelSeconds × fuelMul` (fireplace 1.5, wood_stove 2.0). `fuelMulOf(world, fire, defs)`.
- `windsock` (decor, non-solid, `features.windsock`): no gameplay state. `windsockReading(world)` → `{dir, speed, strength 0..1, stage 0..3}` from replicated core `Weather.windDir/windSpeed` (`WINDSOCK_FULL_SPEED` = 12 tiles/s). Art hook: rotate to `dir`, inflation pose by `stage`.

### 6.2 PvP / clan pieces and Burning (Art & Assets pvp_clan proposal)
Pieces (`pieces.json features`, module `combat/pvp_pieces.ts`, systems `gameplay.contact` every 4 ticks and `gameplay.burning` every fire.checkSeconds, after zombieAI):
- `barricade_spiked` `features.contact {dps, radius, slowMul, slowSeconds}`: creatures in radius take `DamageCause.Contact` damage and a MoveSpeed Mul slow; the piece's own Tribe is spared.
- `raid_bell` `features.alarm {radius, cooldownSeconds}`: any piece damaged within radius → reliable `gameplay.RaidAlarm {bell, piece, tribe}` (per-bell cooldown, server memory only).
- `gate_clan` `features.breachAt` (0.25): at hp/max ≤ breachAt the door goes `DoorState.open = 2` (`DOOR_BREACHED`, Art "breached" state), unlocked, cannot be toggled; repair above the threshold resets it to closed.
- `gate_clan`, `clan_hall_door` `features.clanLock`: door spawns `lockTribe` = builder tribe.
- `watchtower` `features.sightBonus` (12): `sightBonusAt(world, x, y)` hook for vision / AOI (not yet consumed — ask Netcode).
- `banner_wall` `features.clanTint`: BuildPiece.tint = clan palette index.

**Burning** `{lit u8, intensity u8, startTick u32}` (networked, persistent; matches packages/render/src/burning.ts). `ignite(world, e, intensity?)` / `extinguish`. Pieces: only `fire.json flammableTiers`; dps × intensity/255 per check, +growPerCheck, spread within spreadRadius with spreadChance (`world.rngFor("fire.spread", piece)`), rain/storm −rainQuench. Creatures: `DamageCause.Fire`, flame decays by growPerCheck. Events: Ignited/Extinguished (unreliable). STRC does not yet encode Burning (fires are not restored after load).

### 6.3 Foods, spoilage stages, water flask
- New foods (items.json): mushroom, carrot, apple, fish → cooked_fish, wild_grain → bread, stew_bowl (campfire: planks + raw_meat + carrot + mushroom), egg. Gathered as yields of berry_bush / fiber_grass / tree; fish comes from clay_deposit as a stopgap until water tiles exist.
- Four stages are in `environment.spoilage.stages` `{key, minFresh, nutritionMul, sick}`. `src/food.ts` `spoilStageIndex`; eating multiplies the food by the stage's nutritionMul, and a sick stage applies the sickness debuffs. Freshness is still lazy (no per-tick cost).
- Liquid containers: item `sips` (water_flask 4; requires maxStack 1 and durability = sips). Fill = slot durability, so it needs no new replicated or persisted state. Each Use drinks food/sips and keeps the flask; an empty flask does nothing. `fillContainer(world, e, slot, sips?)` is the hook for wells and water tiles (no source exists yet).

## Engine note: economy primitives (from Engine Architect, ARCHITECTURE §19, ADR-074..076)

- Core `Slot.crafterId` (u32 PlayerId) + `sameStack` (item, crafterId, durability). Map your `crafterId` arrays with `arrayFieldsContainer({..., crafterId: "crafterId"})` when registering Inventory/Container with core `Containers`.

- Stalls: either core `TradeStall` + `installStalls/stockStall/withdrawStall/setStallListing/buyFromStall`, or your own `Listings` components calling core `atomicPurchase` (atomic, rollback on failure) and `can()`. Sale events: core `SaleLedger.entriesSince(cursor)`. No balance rules in core.

## 9. Economy (player-driven; trade stalls)

**Land claims (core ClaimRegistry, slice 4).** `installEconomy` adds one permission rule (addPermissionRule). For target 0 + `StallActs.place` (`canAt` from validatePlacement for stall pieces), a NoBuild zone is denied, and so is a claim whose ownerGroup the builder is not in (unless `economy.claims.placeInForeignClaim`). Buying from a stall in a claim not owned by the stall's owner (the clan id itself, or a player who belongs to the claim group) is denied (`forbidden`) unless `claims.tradeInForeignClaim`. Management is unaffected, so owners can still empty or move a squatting stall. Without installPermissions, placement skips the claim check.

**Sign text.** `setStallName(world, actor, stall, text)` needs the `list` permission; command `gameplay.setStallName {stall, len u8, name u8[24]}` is appended after setStallOpen. `sanitizeStallName`: anything outside printable ASCII becomes a space, whitespace is collapsed, the text is trimmed and capped at `economy.stallNameMax` (≤ STALL_NAME_MAX 24). Reads: `stallName`, `stallSoldOut`. soldOut and name live in a separate StallSign component, not in StallHeader. That keeps the StallHeader schema and hash unchanged for existing saves and net; render reads both from the stock entity.

**Rules.** No NPC vendors (`data/economy.json noVendorSources`, tested: every item has a gather or craft source). Prices are `{item u16, count u16}`; `coin` is the conventional currency (anvil: 1 copper_ingot → 10 coin). Offline sales credit the till.

**Listing model (one model): gameplay `Listings`**, not core `TradeStall`. Core's TradeStall (fb7bf56) caps at 8 rows (we need 24), has no enabled flag, pays into the stock container (we need a separate till), and keeps an in-memory ledger. Gameplay uses core `transaction()`, `Transaction.take/add` (crafterId stack key), `can()` and `PlayerNameRegistry`.

**Crafter provenance.** Inventory/Container carry `crafterId u32[N]` (PlayerId, 0 = gathered or anonymous items such as coin, `economy.anonymousItems`). Registered with core Containers as `crafterId: "crafterId"`. Different crafters never stack. Provenance survives every transfer, trade (net purchaseSwap / coreTradeInventory), stall sale, death bag and STRC v2 persistence. `itemInfo(world, e, slot, names?)` → `{itemKey, count, durability, freshnessStage 0..3, crafterId, crafterName}` (names default = the world's PlayerNameRegistry; clients pass `client.names`).

**Pieces** (`pieces.json` `stall: {listings, clanOnly}`): stall_blanket 1×1/4, stall_cart 2×1/8, stall_booth 2×2/12, clan_pavilion 3×2/24 (clan-owned only), market_counter 2×1/6 (clan trading post; clan-owned only; tier 2 cost planks+stone_brick+nails; from assets/stalls/pieces.proposed.json). A clanOnly stall cannot be placed by a player without a clan (BuildFail.NoPrivilege). Attachments: **stock** (Container slot i = listing i, StallHeader, Listings, StallLedger, OwnedBy) and **till** (Container, AttachKind.Till). Plain container deposit/withdraw is locked on both.

| Component | Fields | Scope | Persist |
|---|---|---|---|
| StallHeader | ownerKind u8 (1 player, 2 clan), ownerId u32, open u8, listingCount u8 | all (AOI) | STRC v2 |
| Listings | priceItem u16[24], priceCount u16[24], qty u16[24], enabled u8[24], revision u32 (bumps on every listing/open change) | viewers | STRC v2 |
| StallSign | soldOut u8 (1 = no enabled listing has stock ≥ qty; server-maintained after stock/unstock/listing/sale/spill), nameLen u8, name u8[24] (sign text, printable ASCII) | all (AOI) | STRC v3 (name; soldOut recomputed on load) |
| StallLedger | ring of 16: tick, item, count, priceItem, priceCount, buyerId, crafterId; head, total | viewers | STRC v2 |

**Permissions** (`StallActs` bits through `can()`, rule installed by `installEconomy`): a player-owned stall is managed by its owner only. Clan stalls follow `economy.rankPolicy`: leader and officer can list, price, collect and stock, plus remove for the leader. Members can only stock. Unlisted roles (recruits, outsiders) can only buy, and only while the stall is open.

**Commands** (core CommandHandlers, ids appended after GAMEPLAY_COMMANDS): openStall{stall}, closeStall{}, stockStall{stall, listingSlot, invSlot, count}, unstockStall{stall, listingSlot, count}, setListing{stall, listingSlot, priceItem, priceCount, qty, enabled}, buyFromStall{stall, listingSlot, qty, expectedRevision, expectedPriceItem, expectedPriceCount}, collectTill{stall}, setStallOpen{stall, open}. The buy runs as `validateStallPurchase` (pure) and then `executeStallPurchase(tx, …)` inside one transaction. Rejection reasons: not_stall, stall_closed, out_of_range, forbidden, bad_listing, listing_disabled, price_changed, stale_listing, sold_out, no_funds, no_space, till_full, bad_qty. Buyers in the same tick are serialized in command order.

**Events** (reliable): `gameplay.stall_sale {stall, listingSlot, item, count, priceItem, priceCount, buyerId, crafterId, ownerKind, ownerId}` goes to the owner (or clan) and the buyer. `gameplay.till_collected {stall, collectorId, items, ownerKind, ownerId}`.

**Raiding.** A destroyed stall (anything except demolish) spills its stock and till into LootBags when `stallStockLootable` is set (the default).

### UI data contract (Art & Assets)
- Browse view: StallHeader plus per listing `{slot, stockItem, stock, priceItem, priceCount, qty, enabled}` (`stallListings`), and `revision` for the buy payload.
- Owner manage view: the browse view, plus till contents and the ledger entries `{tick, item, count, priceItem, priceCount, buyerId, crafterId}`, newest first.
- Tooltip: `itemInfo` → itemKey (manifest key), count, durability, freshnessStage 0..3 (fresh/stale/spoiling/rotten, from `environment.spoilage.stages`), crafterName (resolved from crafterId), and `fill` 0..1 for liquid containers (water_flask; absent otherwise).
- Stall visuals: the piece key (manifest), `StallHeader.open` (0/1), and `BuildPiece.tint` = clanPalettes index (`clanTintIndex`).

## 10. Character customization (slice 8)
Core owns `Appearance` + `validateAppearance`/`setAppearance`; Netcode owns `AppearanceServer`; Art & Assets owns the catalog/art.
Gameplay (`src/customization.ts`, `data/cosmetics.json`) owns the *rules*:
- **Unlock tags**: `cosmetics.json.unlockTags` (append-only; index = bit in `Wardrobe.unlocks u32[2]`, max 64). Grants are
  data rules `{tag, on: firstJoin|craft|stallSale|raidBreach, recipe?}`. Wired: firstJoin (first successful look write),
  craft (`CraftCompleted`), stallSale (`StallSale`, player-owned stall, seller online). `raidBreach` is exposed through
  `noteUnlockEvent(world, attacker, "raidBreach")` and gets wired in slice 6, once damage has an attacker.
- **Edit gate** `canEditAppearance(world,e,initial)`: the first-join creator is allowed once (`Wardrobe.created=0`). After that,
  editing is only allowed within `wardrobeRange` of a piece with `features.wardrobe` (new `mirror` piece, id 50); the check uses the spatial index.
- **Commands**: `gameplay.setAppearance {bodyType u8, skinTone u8, parts u16[8], primary u8[8], secondary u8[8]}` and
  `gameplay.equipCosmetic {slot, wearable, primary, secondary}`. `validate` runs the gate plus `validateAppearance` in reject mode with the
  player's unlocks, and the first issue becomes the CommandRejected reason. `handle` writes via `setAppearance`. Install with
  `installCustomization(world, {catalog, handlers})`.
- **Net adapter**: `appearanceServerHooks(world, markDirty)` → `{unlocksOf, allow, onChanged}`. It is structurally compatible with
  AppearanceServer options, since gameplay imports neither net nor assets.
- **UI contract (hooks, no art)**: the creator shows when `Wardrobe.created==0`. Wardrobe UI prompt near a `mirror`. Locked items show
  their tag from `WearableDef.unlockTags` against the networked `Wardrobe.unlocks`. Rejections show the reason string.

## 11. PvP rules (slice 6): `src/combat/pvp.ts`, `data/pvp.json`
- **Death bags**: `gameplay.deathBags` runs right after `gameplay.death`. When a player (Inventory+Survival) dies, every stack moves into a new
  `LootBag{owner PlayerId, despawnAtTick}` + Container at the body, crafter provenance included, and Equipment is cleared. Event `DeathBagDropped`.
  For the first `bagOwnerOnlySeconds`, only the owner can open the bag (`canLootBag`, enforced on OpenContainer → BuildFail.Locked).
  `gameplay.lootBags` (postSim, every 20 ticks) removes a bag once it is empty or reaches despawnAtTick. Stall spill bags (owner 0) stay open to anyone.
- **Respawn**: the command `gameplay.respawnPlayer {}` or `respawnPlayer(world,e)`, allowed `respawnSeconds` after Dead.tick. The spawn point comes from
  `rngFor("pvp.respawn", e)`. The player gets hp = max×respawnHealthFrac, hunger/thirst from data, and full stamina. Dead, Burning and CombatTag are cleared. Event `Respawned`.
  UI hook: a respawn button countdown from `Dead.tick`, with the reasons `too_soon` / `not_dead`.
- **Hit validation**: the server calls `setHitValidator(world, (attacker, target, {reach, coneDeg}) => reason|undefined)`, e.g.
  `const r = validateHit(server, sessionOf(a), a, t, {reach, coneDeg, aim}); return r.ok ? undefined : r.reason`.
  The combat system checks it for PlayerId attackers on the authority before applying damage. A rejection emits `HitRejected` (unreliable). Gameplay does not import net.
- **Combat tag**: player-vs-player Combat damage sets `CombatTag.untilTick` on both players for `combatTagSeconds`. Read it with `inCombat(world,e)`.
- **Raid attribution**: `damagePiece(world, piece, amount, attacker?)` remembers the last attacker in each tick's batch. `PieceDamaged` now carries `attacker`
  (0 = fire/environment), and `onPieceDamaged` receives it. A clan gate that transitions to breached emits `GateBreached{piece, attacker}` and grants the
  attacker the `raidBreach` unlock rule (raider tag, §10).
- Fix: vitals health regen no longer revives a player whose hp reached 0 earlier in the same tick.

## 12. Netcode adapters (`src/net_hooks.ts`, for net 7ed4157, no net import)
- `installStallBuyHook(stallServer)` → `setBuyHook(gameplayStallBuyHook)`. It maps `listing`→`listingSlot` and calls `buyFromStallResult`, which runs one
  core transaction and returns a reason string or `{crafterId}`. Net does the range/open/permission/rate checks first and sends the notifications afterwards.
- Watchtower AOI: `installViewBonus(world, (cid, n) => server.setViewRadiusBonus(cid, n))`. The system `gameplay.viewBonus` (postSim, every 10 ticks) runs on the authority
  and skips replays. It converts `sightBonusAt` tiles to chunks (ceil / CHUNK_SIZE; net caps the value at 2 and holds it 60 ticks before lowering) and only calls on change. A dead player gets 0. It writes no components.

## 13. Spatial queries for PvP pieces
Fire spread, contact victims, raid-bell lookup and `sightBonusAt` now query the shared `SpatialIndex` (@engine/world) instead of scanning every piece.
The search radius is the fire spreadRadius, the contact radius, or the largest alarm radius / sight footprint across all defs (cached per defs object).
Before each query the index is flushed with `update()`, which is idempotent, so pieces placed earlier in the same tick are still found. Results are sorted by entity id, which keeps rng draw order deterministic.
Contact spikes are still found by query, but each spike only checks the victims near it.

## 14. Slice 7: input hooks and touch helpers (`src/input_hooks.ts`, `data/input.json`)
- Bit orders copy `@engine/input` without importing it, and tests pin them. Hold bits are `GameplayHoldBits` = GameplayActions plus Crouch (bit 9) and Charge (bit 10). Edge bits are `GameplayEdgeBits`, in EDGE_ACTIONS order.
  `extendGameplayActions(names)` adds new actions append-only through core `extendActions`.
- `applyGameplayInput`:
  - Analog move counts as moving when |moveX, moveY| > moveDeadzone (0.15). The digital move bits still work as a fallback.
  - A Hotbar1..8 edge queues `EquipCmd{slot}`; if several are pressed, the lowest one wins.
  - Then it runs `addInputHook` hooks, on both peers in the input phase. Hooks should only queue commands.
- Touch (pure, no DOM):
  - `contextCommandFor(world, player, target, defs)` gives the command the context button issues for a target: bag or container → open, node → gather, door → toggle, fire → toggle, creature → attack.
  - `pickContextTarget(world, x, y, r, actionable)` returns the nearest actionable target found through SpatialIndex.
  - `touchPickRadiusTiles(dpr, tilePx, zoom)` covers a 48dp hit target.
  - `touchGhostPoint` moves the placement ghost touchGhostOffsetTiles along the aim direction, upward by default, so the finger doesn't cover it.
- Charge, Crouch, Jump and Reload have bits but no gameplay effect yet. Art & Assets will add the skins.

## 15. Slice 5: queued crafting on core JobQueues (`src/systems/craft_queue.ts`)
- Single timed crafts (`CraftCmd`/Crafter, predicted on the client) are unchanged. Queued crafting is server-authoritative. Recipes come from gameplay's own loader (`gameplayDefs().recipes`).
- `CraftQueue` = core `defineJobQueue("gameplay.CraftQueue", 4)`. It is networked and persistent, and appended at the end of GAMEPLAY_COMPONENTS. `installCraftQueue(world, handlers?)` installs the core Scheduler and JobQueues, plus the simulation system `gameplay.craftQueue`.
- Enqueue checks recipe, station, count (1..50), queue space, and ingredients (dry run). It then removes ingredients x count in one `transaction()` and enqueues.
  Cancel refunds the remaining count in one transaction. On completion the output is added in a transaction with crafterId provenance.
- Each tick the system pauses the head when the station is out of range, or when the next output won't fit one tick before it is due. It resumes the head when that clears.
  If an add still fails at completion, the output goes into the `CraftOwed` component (see §16).
- Commands use core `installCommandHandlers`: `gameplay.queueCraft {recipe, count}` and `gameplay.cancelQueuedCraft {slot}`. They are registered after PVP_COMMANDS and rejected with reasons via core CommandRejected.
- After a load, the server app must call `world.resource(CraftQueuesRes).rearmAll()`.

## 16. Persistent CraftOwed
- `CraftOwed` is a component with `{item u16[8], count u16[8], crafterId u32[8]}`. It is persistent, not networked, and appended at the end of GAMEPLAY_COMPONENTS, after CraftQueue.
  Entries with the same item and crafter merge. If all 8 slots are taken, completion emits CraftFailed(InventoryFull). In practice this can't happen, because a queue head stays paused while anything is owed.
- `gameplay.craftQueue` delivers owed entries in order as space frees up, and removes the component once it is empty. It runs on the authority and skips replays.
- Old saves have no CraftOwed (or CraftQueue) component, so they load as "nothing owed" with no loader change (confirmed by Netcode). The test covers a core snapshot round trip.

## 17. Spawn tables, AI behavior and human-bot steering data (data only)
Gameplay owns data, not loops: there is **no spawn loop and no controller** here. Engine Architect's PopulationSpawner and AgentController consume these tables.
- `data/spawns.json`: `tables.{zombies,animals,humans}` entries have `densityPerChunk`, `biomeWeights` (biome names map to worldgen tile ids), `phaseMul` (night/dawn/day/dusk), `group {min,max,spread}`, `capPerChunk`/`globalCap`, and `modes {sp,mp}`, plus per-mode `densityMul`/`capMul`. Human bots are sp only (the validator enforces this). Zombie entries use `fromZombies: true`, which takes phase rates and kind weights from zombies.json instead of duplicating them.
- `data/ai.json`: per archetype `perception`, `aggro {mode hostile|territorial|defensive|passive, range, leashRange}`, `flee {hpFrac, range}`, `wander`, `attack` and `loot` (item keys are checked against items.json). Zombie stats resolve from zombies.json (`resolveBehavior`).
- `data/bots.json`: five human archetypes (gatherer, builder, trader, raider, loner). Each has trait ranges (bravery, sociability, greed, diligence, aggression, curiosity), a routine template per day phase, goal weights for base/gear/food (summing to 1), imperfection settings (reaction delay in ticks, aim error, idle looks), chat text keys per situation, and utility considerations `{base, inputs}` for gather/craft/build/fight/flee/eat/sleep/trade.
- `src/ai/population.ts`: `validateSpawnData`, `validateAiData` and `validateBotData` return error lists, and `loadPopulationData` throws when data is invalid. Pure helpers for the spawner and controller: `chunkTarget`, `capFor`, `entriesForMode`, `phaseMultiplier`, `routineActivity`, `rollTraits(rand)` (pass a `world.rngFor` rng), `scoreUtilities`.
- `src/ai/bot_input.ts`: `intentToFrame`, `intentSequence` (adds reaction-delay idle frames) and `jitterAim`. These map bot intents to the same InputFrame bits and player commands (Gather, Attack, Craft/QueueCraft, Place, Use, Equip, OpenContainer, BuyFromStall), so bots go through the same validation and rate limits as players.

## 19. Stall display copy (`src/systems/stall_display.ts`)
- `gameplay.stallDisplay` (postSim, every 5 ticks) copies each stall's viewer-scoped Listings into core `StallDisplay`, which is broadcast. installGameplay registers StallDisplay after GAMEPLAY_COMPONENTS. Row i = listing i: item = stock item if enabled (else 0), qty, price = priceCount, currency = priceItem. Writes only on a diff.
- The row count is `fieldLength(StallDisplay.schema.item)` (core STALL_LISTINGS, currently 8). `truncatedStallTiers` lists pieces whose `stall.listings` exceed it: stall_booth (12) and clan_pavilion (24) are cut off until core raises STALL_LISTINGS to 24. The test pins that list.
