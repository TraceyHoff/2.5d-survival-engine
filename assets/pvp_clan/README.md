# pvp_clan: PvP, clan and raid art pack (Cozy-Wild)

Everything here is generated by `python3 tools/build_all.py` (run from this folder). The art is drawn procedurally with Pillow and numpy at 4x supersampling, then LANCZOS-downscaled with premultiplied alpha. Previews are made with `python3 tools/previews.py`. The engine check is `cd /workspace/engine && npx tsx /workspace/assets/pvp_clan/tools/validate_fragment.ts`. It validates the fragment on its own and merged with the pack manifest, checks every referenced file exists, checks for key collisions, and runs validateBuildingSprites against pieces.json plus pieces.proposed.json.
This pack never touches `assets/`, `assets/ui` or `assets/survival_fx` files. Its colors come from `../palette.json`, and its nameplate/UI colors from `../ui/theme.json`.

## Files
- `manifest.fragment.json`: engine manifest v1 fragment (25 atlases, 132 sprites, 29 vfx, 5 tintSets). Merge its arrays into `assets/manifest.json`.
- `atlases/<atlas>.png` + `.json` (TexturePacker JSON hash, normalized `pivot`, 2px extrude padding, at most 2048²). Each atlas also has an `@2x` page listed under `pages[{scale:2}]`.
- `atlases/<atlas>_emissive.png` (same layout; `masks.emissive: true`): pvp_fx, pvp_fx_tall, pvp_loot, clan_banners (totem rune), raid_fx, raid_fire, raid_rubble.
- `atlases/<atlas>_tintmask.png` (same layout; `masks.tintmask`): clan_banners, clan_territory, clan_buildings, clan_gates, clan_structures_large, raid_rubble, nameplate_icons, nameplate_tags, chat_bubbles.
  **R = clan primary, G = clan secondary** (white = both channels = primary). Tinting multiplies the neutral cream/grey trim: `rgb = mix(rgb, rgb*tint, mask)`. `BuildPiece.tint` (u32) is the primary color. Secondary comes from `tintSets.clan_secondary[i]`, or primary for now.
- `textures/`: tileable strips for engine-drawn ribbons (projectile trails, territory edge strip, loot beam, crack tiles s1-s3).
- `clan_palettes.json` (16 curated clan color pairs), `rarity_palette.json`, `outline_colors.json` (self/party/clan/neutral/enemy), `world_ui_colors.json`.
- `pieces.proposed.json`: proposed BuildPieceDefs for Survival Systems (hp and tier values are placeholders).
- `fonts/`: damage numbers and nameplate bitmap fonts (Fredoka/Nunito, OFL).
- `tools/`: generators (`common.py`, `fx_combat.py`, `loot.py`, `clan.py`, `buildings_clan.py`, `raid.py`, `worldui.py`, `previews.py`, plus copies of toon/iso/palette).

## Conventions
- Pivots: characters/FX on the ground use bottom-center. Air FX (hits, bonks) use center (0.5, 0.5). Buildings sit at the **front-most footprint corner** (frame bottom-center): anchor y 0.9375 for the 128-tall 1x1 pieces and 0.96875 for the 256-tall multi-tile pieces.
- Directions run E, SE, S, SW, W, NW, N, NE (8 rows, `dirStride` = columns) for swings and arrow/spear projectiles.
- Lighting and cast shadows are not baked in. Swings, projectiles and FX have no contact shadow. Ground props carry a faint plum contact blob, as the base pack does.
- Building pieces use `frames[rotation][damageStage]`, with stages 0 intact, 1 scuffed, 2 cracked/chipped/leaning planks, 3 nearly broken (missing bricks, planks hanging). `rotationArt: "mirror"` means rotations 0/2 and 1/3 share art (two unique views baked).
- Flags and banners carry `sway {amp px, freq Hz, stiffness}` for the wind shader. The `flap` anim is an optional intrinsic cloth loop.

## Keys (frames @ fps; loop)
**Combat (pvp_swing_a/b 96², pvp_fx 64², pvp_fx_tall 64×96, pvp_projectiles 48², pvp_trails 64×16)**
- `fx_swing_blade|blunt|axe|spear`: anim `swing` 5 @ 20, 8 dirs, no loop. Anchor (0.5, 0.7) on the attacker's feet. Play on anim state 3 (attack).
- `fx_hit_spark` 5@24, `fx_bonk_stars` 6@18, `fx_crit_burst` 6@18, `fx_parry_spark` 5@20, `fx_block_shield` 5@16, `fx_dodge_dust` 6@14, `fx_impact_puff` 6@16, `fx_impact_flash` 3@24. All are one-shots.
- `fx_ko_puff` 12@12 one-shot (puff, spinning stars, ghost wisp rising). `fx_ko_ghost` 6@8 loop. `fx_downed_overlay` 8@10 loop (circling stars over the head). `fx_downed_ring` 8@8 loop (ground ring). `fx_respawn_sparkle` 10@14 one-shot.
- `proj_arrow`, `proj_spear`: 8 dirs, 1 frame. `proj_sling_stone`: 4@16 spin. `trail_arrow|stone|spear`: anchor (1, 0.5), stretch along velocity.
**Loot (pvp_loot 64², pvp_loot_beam 32×128)**: `loot_sack_small|medium|large` 6@6 sparkle loop, `grave_marker` ("here lies", flowers and candle) 4@6, `loot_glow_common…legendary` 6@8 ground glow, `fx_item_drop_bounce` 6@14 one-shot, `fx_loot_beam` 6@8 (tint with `rarity_beam[i]`, additive).
**Clan (clan_banners 64×128)**: `banner_small`, `banner`, `banner_large` (piece `banner`, layer 3; idle plus `flap` 6@10), `banner_wall` (piece `banner_wall`, `flap` and `flap_r1` 4@6), `claim_totem` 6@6 with an emissive rune (piece `claim_totem`, layer 2).
**Territory (clan_territory 64×32 floor diamonds, tintmask)**: `territory_ne|se|sw|nw` (edges), `territory_in_n|e|s|w` (inner corners), `territory_fill`, each 2@2 shimmer. Pick by neighbor mask; anchor at the diamond center.
**Emblems (clan_emblems 32²; plus `clan_emblems_outline`)**: 24 `emblem_*` icons composited onto the banner/totem plaques.
**Nameplates**: `np_crown|star|shield|party_pip|pvp_flag|downed|officer|leader_clan` (24²), `nameplate_neutral|self|party|clan|enemy` (64×24, anchor bottom), `nameplate_clan_tag|party_tag`, plus `bubble_*` chat bubbles and tails (nine-slice-able, 48²). Colors match `ui/theme.json`.
**Base pieces**: `wall_clan_wood`, `wall_clan_stone`, `wall_clan_palisade` (piece `wall`, 1x1, layer 1), `barricade_spiked` (1x1, layer 1), `storage_box_locked` (piece `storage_box`, layer 2), `raid_bell` (layer 2; `ring` and `ring_r1` 6@12), `gate_clan` (2x1, layer 1, building.states `closed|open|breached`), `clan_hall_door` (2x1), `watchtower` (2x2, layer 1).
**Raid**: `crack_1x1|2x1|2x2` (anims `stage1..3`, a multiply-style overlay for pieces that lack bespoke damage art), `fx_hit_wood|stone|dust` 6@16/16/14, `fx_raid_ember` 4@8 (renamed from `fx_ember`, which survival_fx owns), `fx_raid_smoke` 6@8, `fx_repair_sparkle` 8@12 (hammer and sparkles), `fx_fire_small|large` 8@12 loop with emissive, `rubble_smoldering` 6@6 with emissive embers, `fx_collapse_poof` 10@14 (128²).
**VFX entries**: `pvp_*`, `loot_*`, `raid_*` (29). See `manifest.fragment.json` for lifetime, blend and particles.
**Tint sets**: `clan_primary`, `clan_secondary` (16 each), `rarity_color`, `rarity_beam` (common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary), `outline_roles`.
